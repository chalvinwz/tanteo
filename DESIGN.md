# tanteo — design direction

**One line:** a floodlit night court, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Mood

Social padel ends under floodlights. Dark UI by default: deep court-blue surfaces
(padel courts are blue), structural white line-work borrowed from court markings —
thin, exact, load-bearing, never decorative — and a single hot accent: padel-ball
optic yellow, reserved for scores in motion and the current leader.

## Type

Scores are the hero and must read from two meters away: oversized tabular-lining
numerals in one distinctive display face; a plain grotesque for everything else.
Never letter-space lowercase.

## Liveliness dials

- **ENERGY:** high on the scoreboard and live view, low on setup and settings.
- **RHYTHM:** a court-line grid — hairline rules dividing regions the way lines
  divide a court.
- **MOTION:** exactly two animations exist — the score tick (numeral rolls to its
  new value) and the round transition (court cards resort). Nothing else moves.

## Copy voice

Courtside plain talk. Short imperatives ("Add player", "Start round 4"). No
corporate filler. No exclamation marks except at match point. Padel vocabulary
where natural. English for v1; every string externalized so Bahasa Indonesia can
follow.

## Anti-goals

No generic dashboard shell, no bento grid, no gradient hero, no emoji in UI
chrome, no light gray on white.
